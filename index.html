In Memoriam

FormedOklahoma City · 2009 The Loud Years2009 — 2013 Final ShowMarch 22, 2013 PersonnelJames Smith · Tommy McKenzie · Weston Lorance · Charles Whetstone Recorded byKevin Whetstone · Nice People Came · Wrote · ConqueredAnd then, the silence.

The Boom Bang of Oklahoma City (2009–2013) was formed by friends who turned hanging out into a high-volume garage rock, proto-punk, noise band. Through the loud years, they made songs that were fast, weird, and honest, then left the stage after their final show on March 22, 2013. Call it Noise From Beyond The Grave if you want: the songs still hit, and The Loud Years Live On.

Left behind Pizzapocalypse (2009) · Bummer Camp 7″ — 300 pressed (2010) · World War Fun (2011) · the split 7″ with The Copperheads (2011) · Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtlez (2012). Old footage, live photos, and songs that are Too Dead To Quit.
