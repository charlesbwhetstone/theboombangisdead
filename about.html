About

The Boom Bang was a garage rock band from Oklahoma City. This site is an archive of what is left: footage, photographs and recordings.

The dates and genres shown on this site come from its own structured data, which records the band as founded in 2009 and dissolved in 2013, playing garage rock, proto-punk and noise. Its final show was on 22 March 2013.

Unverified: everything beyond those dates, genres, the final show date and the material published here. Nothing else about the band's history, line-up or discography has been confirmed for this page, so nothing else is claimed. [PHOTO CREDIT WORDING]
